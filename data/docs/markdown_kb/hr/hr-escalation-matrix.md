# HR Escalation Matrix

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to HR and the CEO. It contains fictional HR-sensitive process details for permission testing.

## Escalation Paths

| Issue type | First owner | Escalation owner | Review target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payroll dispute | HR operations | HR lead and Finance payroll owner | 2 working days |
| Employee relations complaint | HR business partner | Head of People | 1 working day |
| Legal risk concern | Head of People | External counsel placeholder | Same day |
| Executive employee issue | Head of People | CEO | Same day |

## Documentation Rule

HR must log source, timeline, documents reviewed, decision owner, and next action in the HR case record. Do not put sensitive case details in general tickets.

## Finance Handoff

Payroll disputes can be shared with Finance only after HR removes unrelated medical, performance, or employee relations details.
