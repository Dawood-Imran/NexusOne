# Performance Review Cycle

## Summary

NexusOne runs two formal performance review cycles each year. The mid-year cycle focuses on feedback and development. The year-end cycle includes rating calibration, promotion recommendations, and compensation inputs.

## Timeline

| Cycle | Employee input | Manager review | HR calibration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-year | June 1 to June 10 | June 11 to June 24 | June 25 to June 30 |
| Year-end | November 1 to November 12 | November 13 to November 30 | December 1 to December 12 |

## Employee Preparation

Employees should document goals, outcomes, collaboration examples, customer impact, and learning areas. Evidence should link to Jira, project summaries, support outcomes, or measurable delivery.

## Manager Role

Managers write feedback in HRIS and discuss it with each employee. Restricted calibration guidance is available only to managers and HR.
