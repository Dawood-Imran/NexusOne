# Payroll Calendar

## Summary

Payroll is processed monthly. Employees should keep HRIS bank, tax, and address details current before the cutoff date.

## 2026 Cutoffs

| Region | Monthly data cutoff | Target salary date |
|---|---|---|
| Pakistan | 20th calendar day | Last working day |
| UAE | 18th calendar day | Last working day |
| UK | 17th calendar day | 26th calendar day |
| USA | 15th calendar day | Last business day |

## Changes After Cutoff

Bank changes, tax updates, and unpaid leave adjustments submitted after cutoff may move to the next payroll cycle.

## Payroll Questions

Employees should create an HR ticket for missing salary, incorrect deduction, bank-account issue, or payslip access issue. HR coordinates any finance handoff internally.
