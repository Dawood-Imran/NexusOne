# Performance Feedback Notes

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to managers, HR, executives, and the CEO.

## Summary

Manager feedback should be specific, evidence-based, and tied to role expectations. Notes in HRIS should avoid unsupported claims and should separate performance facts from coaching opinions.

## Good Feedback Includes

- Outcomes delivered.
- Quality, reliability, and collaboration examples.
- Missed commitments with context.
- Growth areas and support plan.
- Clear next expectations.

## Poor Feedback Patterns

Avoid vague labels, surprise criticism, comparisons to other employees, or medical and personal assumptions.

## HR Review

HR may ask managers to revise feedback that lacks evidence, includes sensitive personal details, or conflicts with company policy.
