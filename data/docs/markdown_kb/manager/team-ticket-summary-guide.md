# Team Ticket Summary Guide

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to managers, HR, executives, and the CEO. Department queue owners may have separate tool permissions in the application.

## Summary

Managers can view summary-level status for tickets opened by direct reports when the ticket category supports manager visibility. Sensitive HR details are hidden unless HR explicitly grants access.

## Visible Fields

Managers may see ticket ID, requester, department queue, category, status, priority, created date, and blocker flag. They should not see payroll details, medical documents, employee relations notes, or finance audit notes.

## Common Uses

- Identify onboarding blockers for a new joiner.
- Track device replacement status.
- Review open admin requests for office setup.
- Confirm whether a finance reimbursement claim needs manager approval.
