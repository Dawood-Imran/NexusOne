# Strategic Priorities 2026

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to executives and the CEO.

## Summary

The 2026 strategy emphasizes workflow automation, enterprise renewal quality, regional expansion discipline, and tighter spend governance.

## Priorities

1. Reduce manual customer reporting work by 40 percent through automation.
2. Improve enterprise renewal readiness for Customer A, NexusRetail, and AtlasPay segments.
3. Expand selectively in UAE and UK while monitoring support capacity.
4. Align hiring requests with budget utilization and revenue-risk mitigation.

## Operating Constraints

New headcount and cloud spend require finance review when they affect runway scenarios. Executive hiring is reviewed separately from manager hiring requests.
