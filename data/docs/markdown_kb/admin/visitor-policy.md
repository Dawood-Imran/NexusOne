# Visitor Policy

## Summary

Visitors must be registered before arrival and hosted by an active NexusOne employee. Visitor access is limited to approved office areas.

## Request Steps

1. Open the Admin Portal and create a visitor request.
2. Enter visitor name, company, visit date, office, host, and business purpose.
3. Submit at least one working day before the visit when possible.
4. Ask the visitor to check in at reception with photo identification.

## Host Responsibilities

The host must meet the visitor at reception, keep the visitor in approved areas, and ensure the visitor badge is returned before leaving.

## Restricted Areas

Server rooms, finance file storage, HR case rooms, and executive meeting rooms require explicit Admin approval.
