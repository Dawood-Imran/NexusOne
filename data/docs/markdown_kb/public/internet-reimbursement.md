# Internet Reimbursement

## Summary

Employees working remotely or hybrid may claim a monthly home internet reimbursement when their role requires reliable connectivity for company work.

## Monthly Limits

| Region | Monthly limit |
|---|---:|
| Pakistan | PKR 8,000 |
| UAE | AED 120 |
| UK | GBP 35 |
| USA | USD 45 |

## Required Evidence

Attach the monthly internet bill or provider receipt. The document must show the employee name or home address, billing period, provider, and amount paid.

## Claim Timing

Submit claims by the fifth working day of the next month. Late claims are paid in the following reimbursement cycle unless Finance asks for correction.

## Denial Reasons

Finance may deny claims with missing receipts, duplicate monthly submissions, mobile-only plans without manager approval, or bills that do not match the employee region.
