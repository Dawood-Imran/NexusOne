# Expense Claim How-To

## Summary

Employees submit reimbursable business expenses through the Finance Portal. Claims must include a readable receipt, expense category, business purpose, date, currency, and manager approval when required.

## Claim Steps

1. Open the Finance Portal and choose `New expense claim`.
2. Select the category: travel, meal, internet, equipment, software, training, or other.
3. Upload the receipt or invoice as PDF, JPG, or PNG.
4. Add the business purpose and project code if applicable.
5. Submit for approval before the monthly cutoff.

## Required Receipt Rules

Receipts must show merchant, date, amount, tax if available, and payment confirmation. Screenshots are accepted only when the vendor does not provide a formal receipt.

## Common Limits

- Home internet reimbursement is monthly and region-specific.
- Meals need a business purpose and attendee count.
- A home office monitor needs manager approval before purchase.
- Software subscriptions require IT approval when they process company data.
