# Onboarding Overview

## Summary

New joiners complete onboarding across HR, IT, Admin, and their reporting manager. The expected timeline is five working days, with access requests starting before the first day whenever possible.

## First Week Checklist

1. Confirm HRIS profile, emergency contact, tax region, and payroll details.
2. Set up MFA and SSO before accessing Slack, Jira, HRIS, and the Finance Portal.
3. Review the employee handbook, leave policy, device policy, and security guidelines.
4. Complete department orientation with the assigned manager.
5. Create tickets for missing tools, device issues, or office access needs.

## Ownership

HR owns employment paperwork and policy orientation. IT owns laptop, SSO, MFA, VPN, GitHub, and AWS access. Admin owns office seating, visitor rules, desk equipment, and access cards. The reporting manager owns team expectations and first-week tasks.

## When To Escalate

If a new joiner cannot access HRIS, Slack, or email by the end of day one, open an IT ticket and mark it as onboarding-blocking. Payroll issues should be raised with HR, not Finance, unless HR redirects the ticket.
