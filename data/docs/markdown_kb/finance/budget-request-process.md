# Budget Request Process

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to Finance, executives, and the CEO.

## Summary

Budget requests are used for spend that is not already approved in the annual plan. Managers submit context, but Finance owns final budget validation.

## Required Inputs

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Department | Requesting department |
| Spend category | Hiring, software, travel, vendor, equipment, or other |
| Amount | Requested amount and currency |
| Timing | Quarter and expected payment month |
| Business reason | Delivery, compliance, customer, or operational rationale |
| Owner approval | Manager or executive sponsor |

## Review Criteria

Finance checks budget availability, committed spend, vendor dependency, hiring plan alignment, and revenue-risk impact.

## Fictional Example

Engineering requested USD 48,000 additional cloud budget for Q2 2026 because staging load tests expanded after Customer A renewal planning.
