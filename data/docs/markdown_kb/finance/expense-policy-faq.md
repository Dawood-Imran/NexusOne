# Expense Policy FAQ

## Summary

This FAQ explains common employee-facing expense rules. Finance-only audit notes and exceptions are not part of this public policy.

## Can I Expense A Home Office Monitor?

Yes, if your manager approves the purchase before you buy it and the monitor is needed for remote or hybrid work. The standard reimbursement cap is USD 220 or local equivalent unless Finance grants an exception.

## Can I Claim Meals?

Business meals are reimbursable when they support customer meetings, approved travel, or approved team events. Include attendee count and business purpose.

## Can I Claim Internet?

Yes, monthly home internet is reimbursable within the regional limit if you attach a bill or provider receipt.

## What Gets Denied?

Duplicate claims, missing receipts, personal subscriptions, unapproved software, and expenses submitted after repeated correction requests may be denied.
