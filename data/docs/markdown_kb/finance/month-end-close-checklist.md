# Month-End Close Checklist

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to Finance, executives, and the CEO.

## Summary

Month-end close confirms revenue, expenses, accruals, vendor aging, payroll postings, and management reporting. The checklist uses fictional data categories only.

## Checklist

1. Lock prior-month expense submissions.
2. Reconcile bank feeds and payment confirmations.
3. Review vendor aging and late invoices.
4. Post payroll journal summary from HR-approved payroll file.
5. Validate deferred revenue and customer renewal adjustments.
6. Review department budget variance.
7. Publish management summary to approved finance viewers.

## Close Windows

Finance asks Engineering and Admin to avoid non-urgent vendor or procurement changes during the final two working days of close.

## Exceptions

Material variance, unreconciled payment, or customer renewal risk must be escalated to the Finance lead before close sign-off.
