# Vendor Payment Process

## Access Notice

This document is restricted to Finance, executives, and the CEO. It uses fictional vendors and payment statuses for testing.

## Summary

Vendor payments require invoice validation, purchase approval, tax check, payment scheduling, and reconciliation. Finance tracks aging to avoid service disruption.

## Payment Steps

1. Validate invoice number, amount, currency, and vendor record.
2. Match invoice to purchase approval or contract.
3. Confirm department owner approval.
4. Schedule payment based on terms and cash planning.
5. Reconcile payment confirmation in the finance ledger.

## Fictional Aging Examples

`CloudBridge Labs` has a 21-day software invoice awaiting department owner approval. `Northstar Facilities` has a 34-day facilities invoice pending vendor tax correction.

## Escalation

Invoices older than 30 days require Finance lead review. Any vendor tied to customer delivery should be flagged before payment delay.
